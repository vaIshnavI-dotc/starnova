# Entry rule
A tip is treated as actionable at the OPEN of:
- the same trading day, if posted before 09:15 IST on a trading day;
- otherwise the next trading day.
Reason: avoids look-ahead bias, since a source gets no credit for a move
that happened before the tip could be acted on.
Prices are Yahoo adjusted (splits, bonuses and dividends).
Times are stored in UTC and converted to IST (UTC+5:30) for this rule.
